# Cold-outreach kit — finding gym owners who'll pay $2,000

Three channels in priority order: cold email, Instagram DM, in-person walk-in.
Templates below have been written conservatively — no fake claims, easy to
say no to. The goal is *one good 20-minute call*, not a sale on first contact.

## Where to find them

- **Local SEO**: search `martial arts gym <city>`, `BJJ academy <city>`,
  `boxing gym <city>`. Take the gyms ranking 4th–15th — they'd benefit
  most from better SEO/booking, and the 1st–3rd already have a tech team.
- **Google Maps**: filter by `4.5+ stars`, `independent` (no chain logos),
  `under 500 reviews`. These are mid-size, owner-operated.
- **Instagram**: `#bjjmanchester`, `#muaythailondon`, etc. Look at their
  bio link — if it goes to a generic Linktree or a site with a Wix
  watermark, you've got a candidate.
- **Member testimonials**: when a gym's Google reviews say things like
  "love the gym but their booking system is a mess" — you've got a
  ready-made hook.

## Cold email — first touch

> Subject: Two-tap class booking + free trial form (your site, this week)
>
> Hi {first name},
>
> I noticed {Gym Name}'s site uses {their current setup — Wix / WordPress /
> Mindbody}. I built a complete website + member portal + booking system
> specifically for indie gyms — and it ships today. Trial form, Stripe
> checkout, schedule with capacity & waitlist, member portal, admin
> dashboard with a live feed when leads come in.
>
> Most gyms add 1–3 trial members per week from upgrades like this. At
> {your average price}/month membership, it pays back in ~{calculated weeks}
> weeks.
>
> 20 minutes this week to walk you through what it looks like? I can demo
> on screen-share — no pitch deck, just the live thing.
>
> {Your name}
> P.S. — happy to leave you the listing link to look first:
> {sales-page URL}

**Why this works**: specific (mentions current platform), concrete
(numbers, not adjectives), low-ask (20 min, you control the demo).

## In-person — walk-in

> "Hi, I'm {name}. I'm not a member, sorry — I make websites for gyms
> like yours and I'd love to leave a one-pager with the manager when
> they're free. Should I email it instead?"

Hand the manager a one-pager (the bullet list from
`marketing/listing-copy.md`) printed on good stock. Email follow-up
within 24 hours.

## Pricing posture

- $2,000 is the published list price. Do **not** negotiate on first call.
- If they hesitate: "I can show you the live ROI calculator — if it
  doesn't pay back in under 3 months, I shouldn't sell it to you."
- Discounts: only for genuine multi-gym deals or in exchange for a
  testimonial + before/after metrics.
- Bundle: "$2,000 plus £400 white-glove install" is a *better* offer
  than "$2,000 alone" for non-technical buyers — they want hand-holding.

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We use Mindbody, it's fine." | "Mindbody is great for big chains. For an independent it's £180/month forever — Forge is one-time. Want to see the side-by-side?" |
| "We're on WordPress." | "Same. WordPress is a starting point — Forge is a finished product. Worth 15 minutes to compare?" |
| "We're not technical." | "That's why I offer a £400 install option — you don't touch a terminal. I'll show you a 90-second video of the setup wizard." |
| "How do I know it works?" | "14-day money-back. No questions asked. And the live ROI calculator — let's plug in your real numbers." |
| "Why isn't this more expensive?" | "Because at $5,000 every gym would still buy it from a freelancer instead. $2,000 is the price where buying it makes obvious sense." |
| "Send me a proposal." | "I'll do better — I'll send you the listing link and the ROI calculator. You'll know in 10 minutes if it's a fit. {sales-page URL}" |

## Call structure (20 min)

- **0–2 min**: their gym story. *Listen.*
- **2–7 min**: live demo on screen-share. Submit a lead, show the live
  feed pop in admin. Show the schedule. Show Stripe Checkout.
- **7–10 min**: ROI calculator with their real numbers.
- **10–15 min**: their objections.
- **15–18 min**: pricing + next step. **Always** propose a specific
  next step ("I'll send the link, you decide by Friday — sound fair?").
- **18–20 min**: thank them. Don't oversell. Don't extend the call.

## What to send after the call

- Sales-page URL.
- ROI calculator URL.
- A 90-second Loom video of you submitting a lead and showing it pop in
  admin live (the killer demo).
- One sentence: "Take your time — buy it when you're ready, or don't.
  Happy either way."

That last sentence converts buyers. People hate being sold to.

## Tracking

Keep a simple sheet: `gym name | first contact | replies | demo booked |
sold | reason if not`. After 50 cold emails you'll know which subject
line and which gym profile actually converts. Iterate.
