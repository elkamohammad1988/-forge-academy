What You Get

What A Forge
Membership Includes.

Rather than quotes, here is exactly what the member system does once you join.

01
📅

Class Booking

Book any class on the timetable from your member account. Every class has a set capacity, and the schedule shows how many spots are left before you book.

02
⏳

Automatic Waitlist

When a class is already full you join the waitlist and are told your position. If someone cancels, the next person on the list is confirmed automatically — no need to keep checking.

03
✍️

Digital Waiver

The liability waiver is read and signed online, once per published version. Class and personal-training bookings stay locked until yours is signed.

04
💳

Stripe Checkout & Billing

Membership is paid through Stripe Checkout. The billing portal in your account lets you update your card, view invoices and cancel your subscription yourself.

05
⏸️

Membership Pauses

Request a pause on your membership from your account. Once it's approved, the Stripe subscription is paused alongside it.

06
🥋

Personal Training

Coaches publish their own 1-on-1 availability. Members book and pay for a PT slot directly, and it shows up in both your account and the coach's panel.
