    The Forge Path

    Day One to Black Belt.

    The mat doesn't care where you start — only that you take the next step. Here's the road from your first nervous walk-in to mastery, mapped out.

    1. 0–6m
      01 White Belt

      Step On The Mat

      The first class is the hardest one you'll ever do. We pair you with a beginner buddy, kit you out, and walk you through every drill — no live sparring until you're ready.

      • ✓ Breakfalls & safe movement
      • ✓ Frame, posture & basic positions
      • ✓ Tap etiquette & sparring rules
      ≈ 24Classes
      0→4Stripes earned
    2. 6m–2y
      02 Blue Belt

      Find Your Game

      You're not a beginner anymore. You've earned the right to work moves — not just survive them — and the gym starts to feel like home.

      • ✓ Submissions from every guard
      • ✓ Pressure passing & escapes
      • ✓ Your first competition
      ≈ 300Hours on the mat
      1+Comp medal
    3. 2–4y
      03 Purple Belt

      Sharpen The Edge

      The technical engine room. You're refining your A-game, teaching white belts, and learning that less is more — every move pays for itself.

      • ✓ Advanced sweeps & guard retention
      • ✓ Concept-led problem solving
      • ✓ Coaching newcomers
      ≈ 600Hours on the mat
      50+Classes taught
    4. 4–6y
      04 Brown Belt

      Forged In Fire

      Quiet confidence. Your style is yours. You lead classes, mentor blues and purples, and the way you move is unmistakably your own.

      • ✓ Specialised systems & traps
      • ✓ Leading classes & seminars
      • ✓ Cross-discipline competition
      ≈ 1,000Hours on the mat
      100+Classes led
    5. 6y+
      05 Black Belt

      The Beginning

      It takes half a decade or more to earn. They say the black belt is just the start of mastery — and the people on this mat become your family for life.

      • ✓ Mastery of fundamentals
      • ✓ Generational coaching
      • ✓ Legacy on the academy mat
      ≈ 2,000+Hours on the mat
      ∞Lifetime forged

    Every discipline has its road. Muay Thai grades, boxing levels, kids stripes — your coach maps your path from class one. The belts above are BJJ, but the journey is universal.

    Where you start doesn't matter. Where you choose to walk does.

    First Class Playbook

    Your First 90 Minutes,
    Mapped Out.

    The biggest reason people don't start? They don't know what to expect. Here's exactly what your first session looks like — minute by minute, promise by promise.

    1. −10 min

      Walk In

      Front desk knows your name. We hand you a clean kit if you need one and walk you to the changing rooms.

      Promise No "fill out 5 forms" surprise.
    2. 0 min

      Meet Your Buddy

      A senior member is paired with you. They explain the format, where to put your kit, and what's about to happen.

      Promise You won't stand alone in a corner.
    3. 5 min

      Warm-Up

      Mobility, basic movement, breath work. Coaches scale every drill — if it's too much, you signal and we adjust.

      Promise No burpee shock-and-awe.
    4. 15 min

      Technique Drill

      One technique, broken into three steps. You drill with your buddy, no resistance. Coach circles, corrects, encourages.

      Promise You'll leave knowing one move cold.
    5. 45 min

      Watch & Stretch

      Senior students do positional sparring; you watch and ask questions. Cool-down stretch closes the class together.

      Promise No live sparring on day one. Ever.
    6. +5 min

      Coffee & Questions

      Coach checks in. We answer everything — soreness, schedule, what to wear next time. Then you go home buzzing.

      Promise Zero hard sell. Train when you're ready.

    That's it. No surprises. Bring water, comfortable kit and an open mind — we'll handle the rest.
