  1. Weeks 1–2

    Stance & Strikes

    Stance, basic guard, jab–cross, teep, low kick. On the bag first, then on the pads with a coach who calls every shot.

  2. Weeks 3–6

    Combinations

    3-and-4 piece combos, switch kick, knees on the pads, body work. The cardio sets in. Your shins thank you for the cushion.

  3. Months 2–4

    Clinch & Light Spar

    Clinch fundamentals, elbow strikes, light controlled sparring. 30% intensity, coach-supervised — going harder is opt-in only.

  4. Month 5+

    Full Sparring

    Sparring nights, interclub fight-prep if you want it, conditioning rounds. By month six you're not the same person who walked in.

01

Pads + bags first

You'll spend weeks on pads with a coach before any contact with another beginner. No "get in there and figure it out".

02

Light, controlled sparring

When sparring starts, it's at 30% — coach-supervised, with shins guarded. Going harder is opt-in only.

03

The cardio is unreal

One Muay Thai class equals about three normal gym sessions. You'll be in the best shape of your life inside two months.
