  1. Weeks 1–4

    Survival

    Posture under pressure, breathing, escape from mount, escape from side control, basic guard. You won't tap to anything you didn't ask to.

  2. Weeks 5–8

    First Submissions

    Fundamental sweeps, your first submission (armbar from guard), passing the guard. The week your friends ask why you keep talking about jiu-jitsu.

  3. Months 3–6

    Putting It Together

    Light positional sparring, stripe-check rounds, drilling with intent. You start to recognise positions in real time, not just after.

  4. Year 1+

    Blue Belt Track

    Full sparring, optional competition prep, working towards your first promotion. Most get there by month 14 — at their pace, not ours.

01

No live sparring in week 1

You'll drill, you'll learn, you'll move — but no full-resistance sparring until you're ready. We'll tell you when, not the other way round.

02

Free gi for your trial

We lend you a clean gi for your first two weeks. Most members buy their own once they decide to commit.

03

Zero ego, enforced

Coaches stop sparring rounds the moment they see ego. You will not get smashed by a senior member. Ever.
